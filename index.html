How Costa Works

One Chief of Staff.
An infinite team behind her.

Costa sits between you and your entire operation. She delegates to a team of specialized agents, keeps everything compliant, and surfaces only the decisions that need you.

🧠

One unified client record

Every agent shares a single view of the client, the household, the history, and the compliance file. No silos. No duplication.

🔐

Know Your Agent (KYA)

Every agent has a registered identity, scoped permissions, and a human owner. Compliance is built in — not bolted on.

⚡

Proactive, not reactive

Costa doesn't wait for questions. She monitors, alerts, prepares, and executes — surfacing decisions before they become problems.

🛠️

No-code admin IDE

Admins wire integrations and configure agents visually. No engineers required to connect your existing systems.

📋

Compliance at every step

Every action is checked, archived, and auditable. Reg BI, KYC/AML, suitability — governed continuously, not sampled quarterly.

🤝

Human judgment at the center

Agents handle the work. You make the calls. Costa brings only what genuinely needs your expertise — nothing else.

The Agent Team

Five agents. One mission.
Your time back.

Each agent is purpose-built, compliance-aware, and governed by KYA. Together they cover every function of the advisory practice.

🎯

Acquire

Finds and researches prospects, drafts outreach, books meetings, and runs the entire account opening end-to-end — identity, e-sign, custodian, transfer.

📊

Manage My Book

Preps meetings, writes follow-ups, updates records, monitors portfolios, and handles service requests — before you even ask.

📈

Grow My Book

Spots whitespace and life-event triggers, then builds proposals and referral plans targeted to each relationship.

🛡️

Compliance

Checks suitability and Reg BI, archives all communications, monitors KYC/AML, and runs KYA controls continuously.

🎓

Training

Coaches in the flow of work with micro-learning, role-play simulations, and just-in-time guidance exactly when you need it.
